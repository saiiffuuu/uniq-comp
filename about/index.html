About UniQ Computers

Practical security, professionally delivered

We bring together CCTV equipment, installation, system configuration and support to make security simpler for our customers.

Our approach starts with listening. We learn about the property, the areas that matter most and how the system will be used. From there, we help identify a suitable solution, install it professionally and explain the setup at handover.

Whether you're securing a home, office, shop or other site, our goal is to provide clear advice, quality work and responsive support throughout the process.
